## Lessons

- Two modules agreeing on a field name is a contract nobody tests by default: the planner read pm_risk_level while pm-apply wrote pm_technical_risk for months and every pre-flight silently passed. A shared fixture that round-trips pm-apply output into the planner is the only honest guard.
- Body-wide inference of touches_files turns quoted example paths in AC prose into phantom conflicts (SLICE-51's own AC text made SLICE-47 look conflicting). Declare touches_files explicitly on every slice the loop dispatches; inference is a fallback, not a source of truth.
- Extract helpers while writing the change, not after review: the new sequential guard pushed packWave over the complexity cap because it was added inline next to extracted resolvers.
