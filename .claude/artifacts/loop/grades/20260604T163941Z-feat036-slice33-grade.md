## Lessons

- Existing `parseArchitectOutput` was 6 lines. The hardened version is 14 lines. Defensive parsers do not have to bloat — `hasSemicolon ? splitOnSemi : splitOnNewline` is the whole pivot, the rest is mark-strip.
- Issue #9 fix was a single `||` chain extension in `buildAgentPair` — but the bug had been silent for a month because no test asserted the start-payload-fallback path. The test gap was the bug surface.
- `loop:spec-decompose` parser bug was self-evident the moment I ran it on SPEC-002 (got 1 stub FEAT for a 3-FEAT decomposition). Eat-your-own-dogfood validation works.

## Surprises

- `slice complete` auto-rolled FEAT-036 to `done/` and SPEC-002 to `satisfied` despite SLICE-33 closing only 2 of 5 issues. Feature-lifecycle inference counted SLICE-33 as the only slice for FEAT-036 → all-slices-complete → FEAT done. The system's slice/feature mapping doesn't know about deferred scope.
- SPEC-002 satisfaction therefore overstates completion. AC-4 ("each of the nine issues, an artefact traceable") technically fails for #5/#6/#7. The auto-roll is correct given its inputs; the inputs (slice/FEAT linkage) under-described the deferral.

## Followups

- **File new FEAT for remaining FEAT-036 scope** — issues #5 (skipped docwriter regression test), #6 (gh_unavailable path test), #7 (fetchPrStatus JSON schema). Should be one slice of pure test additions. Reference SPEC-002 AC-4 gap.
- **Improve slice/FEAT deferral signaling** — when a slice closes a FEAT with explicit deferred scope, the close ceremony should either (a) refuse to roll FEAT to done unless deferral is captured as a new FEAT, or (b) auto-create a follow-up FEAT from the slice's "Out of scope" section.
- **Tighten SPEC satisfaction** — `spec-status` should check AC-4-style traceability when present rather than only counting derived_features in done/.

## What went well

Pivoted away from "ship all 5 issues in one slice" the moment slice ACs got crowded. Smaller SLICE-33 finished cleanly in 15 minutes with two independent fixes. Both fixes had test coverage that would have caught the original bug.

The architect parser fix landed with a back-compat-preserving branch (`hasSemicolon ? ... : ...`) so prior callers using semicolon format remain unaffected. Lower regression risk than a full rewrite.

## What went wrong

Forgot to either (a) split FEAT-036 into two FEATs at triage time, or (b) explicitly mark issues #5/#6/#7 as deferred in slice frontmatter so the FEAT lifecycle wouldn't auto-close. Result: SPEC-002 reads as satisfied when 3 of 9 issues are unaddressed.

## What I would do differently next time

When a FEAT has 5+ heterogeneous issues, file 2 FEATs at triage instead of one multi-slice FEAT. Slice/FEAT mapping in loop methodology is cleaner when FEATs are 1-3 slices.
