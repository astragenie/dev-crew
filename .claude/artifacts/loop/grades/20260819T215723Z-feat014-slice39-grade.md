## Decisions

### DEC-036: crew/ keeps scoped typescript@^6.0.3 as a genuine Bun workspace member

**Context**: FEAT-014's flat single-manifest merge cannot hold two versions of one dependency name; openapi-typescript (validate-contracts.mts) hard-requires the TS6 JS compiler API; redocly has no type-gen command; bunx dual-install breaks frozen-lockfile drift guarantees.

**Decision**: Option 1 — root stays typescript@^7/NodeNext, crew/ stays ^6.0.3/Bundler as a workspace member with its own manifest; AC-2 of FEAT-014 means exactly two intentionally-pinned workspace-scoped typescript resolutions, verified via bun pm ls.

**Consequences**:
- Flatten phase 2 is a workspace-ification, not a manifest collapse; the 10 boundary bridges stay permanent (DEC-032).

## Surprises

- The original architect note guessed the coupling was createProgram-class APIs; the real surface is only ts.factory + ts.SyntaxKind — narrower, but equally unservable under TS7.
