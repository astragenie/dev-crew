## What went well

The N>=20 parallel verification gate settled the P1 exit question cheaply and conclusively: 20 crew-suite runs took roughly 5 minutes and came back a perfect 20/20, turning "should be fine" into proof rather than assumption.

## What went wrong

Two of the root-suite runs deviated with an unnamed intermittent failure, but the harness only logged failure detail on deviation rather than every run, costing a 15-run re-hunt to characterize a flake that correlated with heavy parallel agent load on the machine rather than anything in the migration's own change surface.

## What I would do differently next time

Build flake harnesses to capture failing-test names on every run, not just pass/fail counts, so a deviation doesn't require rerunning the whole gate to even identify what failed.
