## Decisions

### DEC-056: Backlog auditor verdict set is closed at three levels

**Rationale**: `likely_satisfied`, `partially`, `open` cover the full spectrum without adding ambiguity. A fourth level would require human judgment to distinguish, defeating the purpose of automated flagging.

**Consequences**:
- positive: simple, predictable output; easy to filter in downstream tooling
- negative: nuanced partial matches can't be graded more precisely without a schema change
