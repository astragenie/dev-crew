## Lessons

- AC text that says "the e2e suite skips/fails" presupposes a wired suite; SLICE-160 proved ASTRAMEM_E2E gating at the helper level only (no memory-e2e suite imports it yet) — verify consumer wiring, not just resolver correctness, before claiming suite-level ACs.
- Double-bound a network timeout: an AbortSignal-driven abort timer AND an independent Promise.race hard fallback (timeoutMs+500ms) so even a fetch seam that ignores the abort signal cannot hang — the exact 300s-hang failure mode this slice guards was reproduced live by the dispatched agents' own astramem calls.
- memory-doctor.mts::probeMemory() (CLI doctor --json) never reports true daemon reachability; test-infra needing real reachability must GET /health directly — the two probes are complementary, not duplicates.

## Surprises

- The astramem daemon flapped fully down mid-slice: all three dispatched agents' agent_profile/recall_memory/remember calls hung 300s or errored, while the same calls were instant earlier in the session. Agent-facing memory was effectively unusable for pm/builder/reviewer.

## Followups

- Wire an actual ASTRAMEM_E2E-gated memory-e2e test that imports preflightAstramemDaemon()/astramemE2eMode() so AC-4/AC-5 are proven at the suite level (the MEDIUM review finding).
- Daemon stability: recall_memory/remember hang 300s intermittently while agent_profile/dashboard also flap (astramem-local #343/#347) — blocks the agent-facing memory loop.

## What I would do differently next time
