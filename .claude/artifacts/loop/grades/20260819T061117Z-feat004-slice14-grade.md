## Decisions

### DEC-014: Legacy dispatch aliases keep historical names; devcrew:* accepted via primary path

**Rationale**: The guard's allowlist exists to tolerate genuinely-old in-flight strings for one release; renaming them defeats its purpose.

**Consequences**:
- positive: in-flight dispatches don't false-block through cutover
- negative: allowlist must be emptied one release post-cutover (P5 note)

## Surprises

## What went well

The command map matched the plan exactly (39 first-party + 1 vendored, zero TBD cells) and the merged commands preserved both parents' real steps verbatim; the reviewer's conditional-approval-then-fix cycle turned around in about 20 minutes once the two real defects were identified.

## What went wrong

The devcrew token sweep was semantically blind and mangled two spots whose entire point was naming OLD tokens: `commands/brief.md`'s history section collapsed two distinct historical command names into the same string, and `hooks/guard-feat-dispatch.mjs`'s legacy-alias allowlist got its own tolerated old strings renamed, silently defeating the guard's purpose of not false-blocking in-flight dispatches.

## What I would do differently next time

Give sweep prompts an explicit "historical-reference" exclusion category, not just fixture/archive exclusions, so prose and allowlists that exist specifically to name old tokens aren't rewritten out from under themselves.
